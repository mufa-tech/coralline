Which one first?

Fix alkalinity first, then calcium, then magnesium. Alkalinity swings hurt corals fastest, and calcium and alkalinity only stay stable when magnesium is in range (about 1250 to 1350 ppm).

Test salinity before you chase any of them. A tank at 32 ppt instead of 35 reads about 9 % low on every element, and topping off the salinity fixes that without a single dose.

These are estimates. Test kits have tolerances and every tank uses a little differently: dose in parts and test again before the next dose.
