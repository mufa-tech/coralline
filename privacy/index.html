Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Short version: Coralline collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Coralline has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. It contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What stays on your phone

Your tanks, tests, doses, products, reminders and settings are saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. We can’t see them.

If you make a backup file or export a CSV, your phone’s share sheet or file picker sends it where you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive). Those services handle the file under their own privacy policies. We never receive it. The same goes for a share card image.

Network

Coralline makes no network requests of its own. The only exception is purchases: buying or restoring Pro is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their privacy policies. We don’t receive your payment details. The app only gets the purchase status from the store and saves it on your phone.

Permissions

The only permission Coralline asks for is notifications, and only when you save your first reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no push service is involved. No camera, location, contacts, microphone or photo access.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to answer you. We don’t share them or add you to a mailing list. Ask at any time and we delete them.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Coralline is made for people who keep reef aquariums. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no app data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. To delete everything in the app, use Settings → Delete all data or uninstall it. For emails you sent us, contact us and we delete them. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Contact: hello@mufatech.com

Coralline is a water test log and dosing calculator for reef aquariums. No cookies or trackers on this site.
